Every rectangle is one of 126 occupation groups (3-digit NCO-2015) from PLFS, India's official labour force survey, for 2025. Size is the number of workers. Colour is the layer you pick below. Hover a tile for details.

How the AI exposure score works
A language model reads the official NCO-2015 description of each 3-digit occupation group, the titles of its 4-digit unit groups, its status mix, and its informality share. It rates exposure on a 0 to 10 scale: - 0-1: almost entirely physical or hands-on work. AI has little effect. - 2-3: mostly physical or interpersonal work. AI helps with peripheral tasks only. - 4-5: a mix of physical and knowledge work. AI assists with some parts. - 6-7: predominantly knowledge work. AI tools are already useful. - 8-9: almost entirely digital work. The occupation faces major restructuring. - 10: routine information processing that AI can already do. The India rubric also weighs the cost of automation against the prevailing local wage and the share of informal work in the group. Scores are model estimates, not predictions of job loss.

Caveats

  • This is not an official statistic.
  • It shows one period. It shows no trend and no projection.
  • It reports the education workers hold, not the education a job requires.
  • Self-employment earnings overstate labour income.
  • Exposure scores are model estimates, not predictions of job loss.
  • Narrow filters (for example 60+ and female) leave many groups with small samples.
